Static electricity can damage sensitive computer components. To prevent this, ground yourself by touching a metal object before handling components, and avoid working on carpeted surfaces. Use an anti-static wrist strap for added protection.

Dust buildup inside a computer can cause overheating and hardware damage. Regularly clean the fans, vents, and components using compressed air. Always turn off and unplug your computer before cleaning to maintain its performance and prevent damage.

Spilling water on a running computer can cause a short circuit and permanent damage. If this happens, immediately unplug it, avoid turning it on, and have it professionally inspected and cleaned before using it again.
